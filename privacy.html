Privacy Policy

Clearframe Publisher · Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. What the Tool accesses

After the TikTok account owner signs in and approves the permissions, the Tool may access:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name and profile picture (user.info.basic).
  • Posting options of the account, such as nickname and the visibility settings TikTok allows (video.publish).
  • The ability to upload videos as drafts (video.upload) or publish them (video.publish), and to check the status of those uploads.

2. How the information is used

The information is used only to show which TikTok account is linked, to upload the videos that the account owner chooses, and to display the status of those uploads. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics.

3. Storage and sharing

The Tool runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored locally on that computer. The Tool operator does not run servers that receive or store TikTok user data. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, other than sending requests to TikTok's own services.

4. Retention and deletion

The account owner can unlink their account at any time inside the Tool, which revokes the access token and deletes it from the computer. Access can also be revoked from TikTok's app permission settings.

5. Children

The Tool is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18.
